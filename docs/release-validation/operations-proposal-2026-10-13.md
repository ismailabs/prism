## Recommended operating choices

| Decision | Proposed operation | Evidence or condition |
|---|---|---|
| Intended initial scope | Adults using a free personal, noncommercial finance-recording service; no bank connection, custody, payment, investment trading or financial-advice service | Matches the implemented recording/planning model. The owner must confirm actual intended audience and commercial status; do not invent either or assume the hosting terms permit a different use. |
| Active application records | Keep user-entered records while the account is active; provide existing confirmed self-deletion. Do not introduce automatic inactive-account purges | Preserves legitimate existing users and records. Applicable legal holds, rights handling and final wording require actual review. |
| Backup retention | Proposed rolling thirty-day encrypted recovery history, plus separately retained recovery key and minimized deletion references needed for re-erasure | Proposal only: current evidence packets remain intact. Do not purge existing evidence or erase keys on this proposal alone. Reviewer must accept the period and any applicable exceptions. |
| Recovery objectives | Proposed daily capture, maximum accepted snapshot age twenty-four hours; propose a one-business-day recovery target while recording actual operator availability | Requires a named person who can actually perform captures and recoveries using existing tooling. No daily schedule is installed, no automatic service is running, and measured platform-independent RTO is not claimed. If this schedule cannot be met, public launch needs an honest alternative accepted objective or a safe free mechanism. |
| Migration/change protection | Capture/rehearse affected data/Auth/provider coverage before production mutations; use bounded locks and additive receipts, then exact-money and ownership checks | Completed for the actual upgrades. A successful prior rehearsal is not authority for an unrelated destructive change. |
| Recovery sequence | Restore into an isolated compatible target, reconcile original amounts/ownership/Auth/MFA, apply post-point legitimate activity and deletion re-erasure before returning service; prefer forward repair | The actual compatible database/Auth restoration passes. Do not restore an old snapshot over newer legitimate transactions. Original-session signing continuity is not promised; require sign-in again when needed. |
| Release monitoring | Review existing Vercel deployment/build status, Supabase Auth/database errors and real synthetic health; retain only sanitized outcome classes, build SHA, time and receipt references | Existing free dashboards and helpers are available. Alert delivery/escalation requires an actual monitored contact; no paid monitoring or unapproved customer telemetry is introduced. |
| Immediate containment trigger | Suspected cross-user access, inconsistent accepted financial amounts, loss of persisted saves, broken Auth/recovery or unknown migration drift stops further release/cutover work and invokes the assigned incident lead | Preserve evidence and existing records. Use the smallest authorized access/write containment; do not delete customer data or perform a destructive rollback. |
| RM0 quota control | Use included capacity only. GitHub's observed $0 Actions budget has Stop usage enabled; preserve required logs and existing artifacts. Review provider usage before launches and at the agreed cadence; pause new registration before reaching relevant free service/email limits | Actual allowance/spend observations are dated in the execution record. Do not infer today's usage from older counts, purchase capacity or promise unlimited public demand. Exact threshold/cadence must match the named operator's real monitoring ability and current provider allowances. |
| Support and privacy requests | Use an owned monitored email; accept an opaque reference, app/device/language and safe error class. Never request passwords, MFA seeds, bearer tokens or complete financial screenshots. Verify ownership before exposing, exporting or deleting account data | Contact ownership, response availability and delivery test remain required actual facts. |

The operator will implement the approved privacy/terms wording and perform technical delivery/monitoring checks when the genuine contact and owner facts are supplied. A proposed role can be held by the same qualified responsible person; no separate paid staffing or invented reviewer is required. The owner must identify who actually accepts each role and the availability they can provide.
